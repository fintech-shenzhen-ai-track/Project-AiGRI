4.1 Flood. Loss or damage to the insured crop caused by inundation from rain, river overflow or levee failure, where water covers the crop for at least three (3) consecutive days.

4.2 Drought. Loss caused by lack of water where the irrigation supply to the insured parcel fails for twenty-one (21) consecutive days or more, as certified by the local irrigation office.

4.3 Pest and disease. Loss caused by an outbreak of pests or crop disease that is not controllable by normal good farming practice.

6.2 Deductible. Losses below ten percent (10%) of the sum insured are not payable.

6.3 Loss categories. A total loss of ninety percent (90%) or more is paid at one hundred percent (100%) of the sum insured. A partial loss between ten and ninety percent is paid at the assessed loss percentage.
