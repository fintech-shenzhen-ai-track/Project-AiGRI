Common questions

Am I covered if a levee breaks?

Yes. Flood from river overflow or levee failure is covered when water covers the crop for 3 days or more.

Is drought covered if I have irrigation?

Only if the irrigation supply to the parcel fails for 21 days in a row.

Is fire covered?

This policy doesn't say. AiGRI can't confirm fire cover. Ask Asuransi Jasindo before you buy. Not stated in this policy
