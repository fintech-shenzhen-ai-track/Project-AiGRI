# AiGRI logo: usage guide

**The idea:** rows of a field with a sun rising at the corner. AiGRI sees what is coming. The sun returns as the dot of the "i" in the wordmark.

## Files

| Use | File |
| --- | --- |
| Website header, documents | `aigri-horizontal-color.svg` |
| On green or dark backgrounds | `aigri-horizontal-reversed.svg` |
| Square spaces, social avatars | `aigri-stacked-color.svg` |
| Symbol alone, 32 px and up | `aigri-symbol-color.svg` |
| Symbol alone, under 32 px | `aigri-symbol-small-color.svg` (two rows, no cut) |
| One colour (print, stamps, embroidery) | `aigri-*-black.svg`, `aigri-symbol-green.svg`, `exports/horizontal/*-white.svg` |
| Favicon, app icons, web manifest | `exports/symbol/` (paste `head-snippet.html` into the page `<head>`) |

## Colours

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field green | `#557945` | 85, 121, 69 | Symbol rows, brand surfaces |
| Sun yellow | `#FFCB3D` | 255, 203, 61 | The sun only, and "Get covered" buttons |
| Ink | `#1F2A1B` | 31, 42, 27 | Wordmark on light backgrounds |
| Leaf green | `#5D9253` | 93, 146, 83 | Illustration only, never the logo |
| Cream | `#FFF8E1` | 255, 248, 225 | Light background |

CMYK and Pantone values are not set yet. Match them with your printer from the HEX values.

## Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep a space equal to one row's height (about 17% of the symbol height) clear on every side.
- **Minimum size:** horizontal lockup 120 px wide on screens, 30 mm in print. Below 32 px, use the small symbol.

## Backgrounds

- **Light (white, cream):** the colour version.
- **Field green or darker:** the reversed version, with white rows and wordmark and a yellow sun.
- **Photos:** only on a dark, calm area of the photo, and only the reversed version.

## Don't

- Recolour the sun or the rows, or swap their colours.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows or gradients.
- Put the yellow-sun colour version on yellow or busy backgrounds.
- Rebuild the wordmark in a font. Always use the supplied files.
- Move the sun or change the gap between the sun and the rows.

## Open items

- Get a professional trademark search before public launch.
- The wordmark letters were drawn for AiGRI, so no font licence is needed.
- Set print colour values (CMYK and Pantone).
