# Logos

The mark is rows of a field with a sun rising at the corner. The sun returns as the dot of the "i" in the wordmark. Use these files as supplied; never rebuild the wordmark in a font. Keep clear space of one row height (about 17% of the symbol height) on every side. Use only through `<img>`, which cannot inherit `currentColor`.

- `aigri-horizontal-color.svg` Field green rows, sun yellow sun, ink wordmark. Use on white or cream in website headers, the desktop sidebar (41px tall) and documents. Minimum 120px wide on screen, 30mm in print.
- `aigri-horizontal-reversed.svg` White rows and wordmark with a yellow sun. Use on field green, ink or a calm dark area of a photo (sign-in hero, 48px tall). Never on a busy or light area.
- `aigri-stacked-color.svg` Symbol over wordmark in the colour palette. Use in square spaces and social avatars, on white or cream.
- `aigri-symbol-color.svg` The symbol alone in colour. Use at 32px and up, such as the mobile top bar (32px).
- `aigri-symbol-small-color.svg` Two rows and no cut, for sizes under 32px (favicons, tiny marks). Same colours as the symbol.
- `aigri-horizontal-black.svg` Single ink, `#000000`. Use for one-colour print, stamps and embroidery on light grounds. It carries its own ink, so it does not change with CSS `color`.

Do not recolour the sun or rows, stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, move the sun or change the gap between sun and rows, or place the colour version on yellow or a busy photo. The logo sun is one of two places `sun` appears; the other is the primary button.
