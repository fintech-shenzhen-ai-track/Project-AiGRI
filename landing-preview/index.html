Satellite checks

Seen from space. Backed by your photos.

After a storm, radar satellites check each of your fields, even through cloud. The readings go in your claim beside your photos.

Radar sees through cloud

Storm cloud hides fields from cameras. Radar still sees the water.

Before and after, per field

Each field gets a flood reading and a crop-change reading, with a confidence level.

Never the final word

Radar can miss flooding under dense crops. A no-flood reading never closes a claim. Your photos and the adjuster fill the gap.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

[Pricing to be decided.]

Who decides my claim?

The insurer's adjuster, always. AiGRI prepares and files your case. It never approves or denies a claim.

What is satellite evidence?

Readings from public satellites showing whether each of your fields flooded and how the crop changed, with a confidence level. It supports your claim. It does not replace your photos or the adjuster.

What if my photo does not pass a check?

The app tells you what looked wrong and asks for a clearer photo. A failed check flags your claim for the adjuster. It does not reject it.

Who can see my farm data?

An insurer sees your data only after you buy from them, and only for the assets that policy covers. You give consent for each insurer at purchase.
